# CodeAlpha Task 1 — SavorLane

- Product listing and individual product pages: `/products/` and `/products/<slug>/`
- Shopping cart: session-backed bag with add, update, remove, and subtotal
- Checkout and order processing: authenticated demo checkout, delivery/pickup, saved order lines, stock update
- User accounts and authentication: registration, login, logout, customer-specific order history
- Database: SQLite with Django models and migrations
- Responsive frontend: mobile navigation and flexible food catalog layouts

Developed by Nadakudhuti Rupasri. Fictional South Indian menu and orders; no real payment or delivery.
